# Veritas Layer Brand Guidelines

## Positioning

Veritas Layer should feel like institutional financial infrastructure with deep engineering credibility.

Core attributes:

- Institutional
- Technical
- Modular
- Secure
- Minimal

## Tone of voice

- Precise over promotional
- Technical over trendy
- Confident over loud
- Systems-oriented over consumer-facing

## Color tokens

- Background: `#060816`
- Surface: `#0F172A`
- Border: `#1E293B`
- Primary: `#4F46E5`
- Accent: `#22C55E`
- Secondary accent: `#06B6D4`
- Heading: `#F8FAFC`
- Body: `#CBD5E1`
- Muted: `#64748B`

## Typography

- Headings: `Inter`
- Product UI: `Inter`
- Technical labels and code: `JetBrains Mono`

## Visual direction

- Dark neutral base with disciplined accents
- Thin-line iconography and systems diagrams
- Measured animation used to stage content, not decorate it
- Modular cards, grid structures, and architectural framing

## UI rules

- Prefer dense but readable layouts over oversized marketing spacing
- Use gradient and glow effects sparingly and only to reinforce information hierarchy
- Highlight compliance, privacy, and architecture equally in above-the-fold content
- Avoid meme-coin, neon-heavy, and speculative crypto aesthetics
