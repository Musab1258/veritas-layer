# Disclosure Policy

## Responsible disclosure

Security findings should be reported privately first through the process in [SECURITY.md](../../SECURITY.md).

## Researcher expectations

- avoid public disclosure before a fix is available or maintainers approve publication
- avoid accessing other users' private data
- avoid disrupting services or settlement flows
- stop testing once you have demonstrated impact

## Maintainer expectations

- acknowledge reports promptly
- keep reporters informed about validation status
- coordinate remediation before publication
- credit researchers when appropriate and requested

## Publication

After remediation, maintainers may publish:

- a summary of the issue
- affected components
- the fixed version or commit range
- any migration or rotation steps required
